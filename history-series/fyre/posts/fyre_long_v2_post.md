At twenty-two, he launched a members-only card for rich young people, promising exclusive parties and VIP access. He loved the lifestyle. Fast cars, fancy dinners, and always the next big idea. And his next idea would make him famous, for all the wrong reasons.

**app:** He teamed up with the rapper Ja Rule. Together, they built an app called Fyre, to let anyone book celebrities for their parties. To make the app famous, Billy came up with a wild plan. They would throw a music festival, on an island, so exclusive and so beautiful that everyone on earth would be talking about it.

**promo:** They flew the biggest supermodels in the world to a beach in the Bahamas. Bella Hadid. Emily Ratajkowski. Hailey Baldwin.
