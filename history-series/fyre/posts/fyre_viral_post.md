## 1. What was going wrong (diagnosis)

| Problem | What the old videos did | Why it kills views | Fix in the new version |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weak first second** | Fyre Part 1 opened on a dark, slow crowd shot with "They promised the party of the century" (a generic line). | On Shorts, people decide in ~1 second. Nothing in frame 1 made them stop scrolling, so YouTube saw people swiping away and stopped showing it. | Frame 1 = the sandwich + a big yellow **"$12,000 FOR A SANDWICH?!"** sticker, and the first line is the shock: *"This cheese sandwich cost twelve thousand dollars."* |
| **Split into "Part 1 / Part 2"** | Every Short had a "PART 1/2" badge and ended with "Follow for part two". | A stranger in the feed won't watch half a story. Part 2 is useless without Part 1, so both get swiped. | One complete, standalone story. No part badge. |
| **No loop** | Ended on a "follow" card. | Replays are a big signal on Shorts. | The last line ("And it all ended with this.") flows straight back into the first line, so the video loops seamlessly. |
| **Slow pacing** | 55 s, long sentences, some scenes 4–5 s. | Viewers drift away mid-video, so few watch to the end. | ~44 s, a new scene every ~2 s, voice ~13% faster and more dramatic. |
| **Small, faint captions** | Captions sat at the top, small, with greyed-out words. | Most people watch muted at first, so they need to read the story fast. | Huge white uppercase captions with a black outline, 3 words at a time, with the spoken word in yellow. |
| **Covers / thumbnails** | Dark gradient + "PART 1" + vague text ("The party of the century"). | It doesn't say *what's shocking*. | A bright, simple promise: **$12,000 sandwich?!** and **PROMISED vs REALITY** (island vs sandwich). |
| **Titles** | "… (Part 1) #shorts". | "Part 1" tells people it's incomplete. | Curiosity titles built on the shocking number (below). |
| **Posting pattern** | Many uploads at once on a small channel. | YouTube tests each new upload with a small audience. Several weak uploads in a row teach it your audience swipes away. | Post one strong Short, wait 24–48 h, read the analytics, then post the next. |

**About the niche:** "rise and fall / scam stories" is a proven niche on YouTube, so the niche isn't the problem. But the big channels in it win on the hook and the pacing, and that's where these videos were weak. The 3D style is fine as long as the first frame and the story hit hard.

**What I can't see:** your YouTube Studio numbers. If you send screenshots of **"Viewed vs. swiped away"** (Shorts) or **impressions click-through rate + average view duration** (long video), I can tell you exactly which fix mattered most. Nobody can guarantee a video goes viral. These changes fix the things that were holding these videos back.

## 4. Posting checklist
- Post the Short alone. Don't post the old Fyre Part 1/2 Shorts alongside it.
- Post when your audience is online (Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube"). If you have no data yet, try 6–9 pm in your main viewers' time zone.
- Reply to the first comments quickly, and pin the question comment.
- After 48 h, check "Viewed vs. swiped away". Above ~70% viewed means the hook works, so do the same structure for the next story.
- Next stories to remake the same way, each with its own one-line shock as the opener:
  - FTX: "He was worth $26 billion in shorts and a T-shirt"
  - WeWork: "He trademarked the word 'We'"
