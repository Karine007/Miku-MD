**Description:**
This desert village hasn't seen rain in months, yet it has fresh, cold water every day, without a single pump.

The secret is a qanat, an underground tunnel system invented in ancient Persia:
⛏️ A deep "mother well" is dug at the foot of the mountains, down to groundwater
🕳️ A tunnel runs from the village to the well, with vertical shafts every few dozen metres
📐 The tunnel slopes down gently, so gravity alone moves the water
☀️ Deep underground, the sun can't evaporate it
🌴 It comes out in the village and turns the whole place green

Some qanats are still running today, and the Persian qanat is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
