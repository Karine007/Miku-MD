**Description:**
It's 40°C in the desert, and he's eating a bowl of ice, centuries before refrigerators existed.

This is the yakhchal ("ice pit"), an ancient Persian ice house:
❄️ In winter, shallow pools freeze overnight in the shade of a huge wall
🧱 Workers stack the ice in a deep pit, packed with straw
🏛️ Walls up to 2 metres thick, made of sarooj mortar (sand, clay, lime, ash, goat hair and egg whites)
🌬️ The tall cone lets hot air escape at the top while cold air sinks to the ice
💧 Meltwater drains away, so the rest stays frozen all summer

Some of these domes are still standing in Iran today.
