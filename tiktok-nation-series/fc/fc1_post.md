## How the series works (our rules)

- 1 follow = 1 fan in the stand. Day 1 starts at 0 fans; the FANS counter shows new followers from now on.
- Comment "OG" = an OG season ticket, with your name on a seat (names go on the seats in Day 2).
- The club starts with 1,000 coins, and every like adds 1 coin. Spend them on what the top comment says.
- Every day the top comment makes one decision, and the stadium on the new island grows step by step.
